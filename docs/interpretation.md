# Interpretation and limitations

The final analysis did not show improved remission prediction after adding eight Bayesian PRS to the clinical-plus-ancestry-PC comparator. This is a negative incremental-prediction result, not evidence that genetic variation is irrelevant to antidepressant response.

The central source-data limitation is that the supplied genotype matrix lacked coordinates, nucleotide alleles and embedded participant identifiers. Allele orientation was inferred and participant linkage depended on the supplied row-order correspondence. Exact round-trip validation demonstrates faithful implementation of the chosen mapping; it cannot independently recover unavailable source metadata.

The study uses internal repeated nested cross-validation in one supplied cohort. It does not establish external validity, clinical utility, dosing guidance or universal cross-ancestry portability.
